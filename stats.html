        How counting works

        The numbers are anonymous. Visitors get no cookies, there are no third-party analytics, and nothing follows anyone to other sites.

        • A visit is one load of the CV page. Reloading it, or coming back within 30 minutes, doesn't count again.
        • CV downloads are clicks on Download CV, with the same 30-minute rule.
        • Visitors are counted day by day, so someone who visits on three different days counts three times in the visitor total.
        • No IP addresses or browser details are stored. To spot repeat visits, the server keeps a scrambled code made with a random key that changes every day, and deletes both within 48 hours.
        • All that's kept for each visit is the country, the type of device, and the kind of site the visitor came from (such as LinkedIn or a search engine), never the page itself.
        • Bots, crawlers and link previews aren't counted. Days follow UK time.

        Your own visits

        Switch this on so your own visits don't inflate the numbers. It saves a cookie and a small flag on this browser only, which is the only time this site stores anything.
